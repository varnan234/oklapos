#06 — Our story

Fifty years on the other side of the counter.

Robbie Stephenson founded OKLA-POS in 2013. Before that he spent decades owning and running restaurants, from quick-service joints to full-service dining rooms.

That is why the setup looks the way it does. A POS should fit the way a kitchen and a floor actually work on a Friday night, not the other way around. Workflows get built around the people using them.

Before OKLA-POS, Robbie spent years in transaction technology at CRS of Oklahoma, a POS dealer formerly based in Oklahoma City. The company started in the OKC metro. In 2022 the main office and operations moved to Chickasha, so a small team could serve small and mid-size businesses across rural and metro Oklahoma.
